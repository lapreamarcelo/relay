# Relay device model credits

These models are independently licensed assets, separate from Matte. Their original embedded metadata and file hashes are retained in ATTRIBUTION.json. Relay aligns/replaces the display texture with the user's media and optionally changes metal finish or hides the supplied band at runtime. The original GLB files are unmodified.

- **Apple Watch Ultra 2** — polyman (https://sketchfab.com/Polyman_3D). Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/apple-watch-ultra-2-f33263c457664b43909200c5ed5e6fa2 . Licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Supplied woven band, optionally hidden.
- **iPhone 15 Pro Max** — MpPower™ (https://sketchfab.com/MG990). Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/iphone-15-pro-max-5b7b35513a154ac69619dc2b2fe15686 . Licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Credit taken from embedded model metadata.
- **Galaxy S25 Ultra** — mistJS (https://sketchfab.com/mistjs). Distributed through https://github.com/YUZU-Hub/appscreen with its README credit identifying CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

Other device families use Relay's original generic geometry; their names do not claim an exact manufacturer model. Draco decoders are from Three.js's glTF distribution of Google Draco, licensed under Apache 2.0 (draco/LICENSE and draco/README.md). Three.js is MIT licensed. DejaVu font licenses are included separately in lib/label-fonts.
